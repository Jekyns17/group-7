FEATURE ESSAY • CAMPUS PERSPECTIVES

Finding Solace Between the Pages: Why Fiction Matters in a Digital Semester

By Daniella Evelyn • Makerere University, Kampala

When university deadlines pile up and coursework assignments fill every waking hour, it is remarkably easy to forget the quiet power of stepping away. Across our lectures and computing labs, screens demand uninterrupted focus. For me, the antidote has always been opening a physical novel.

Reading fiction is not simply a leisure activity; it is an exercise in empathy and imagination. Immersing oneself in intricate psychological narratives and unexpected world-building stretches the mind in ways that technical textbooks cannot.

It sharpens analytical perspective, enriches personal vocabulary, and cultivates patience. Each book invites you to step inside another person's shoes, understand their dilemmas, and navigate complexity.

"A reader lives a thousand lives before he dies. The person who never reads lives only one."

Collaborating with our Group 7 teammates has reinforced that every individual brings a unique creative rhythm to the table. Balancing technical deliverables with literary reflection has kept my semester grounded.
